# PrZ3 Brand Marks

## Parsey (Penguin Parser)

Parsey is the mascot and symbolic representation of the PrZ3 / MSRBot ecosystem.

Role:
- Represents automated parsing and registry maintenance
- Appears in UI, documentation, and automation references
- Associated with the "Penguin Parsing Protocol v3-Gen"

Usage guidelines:
- Use the canonical SVG version when possible
- Maintain original proportions
- Do not recolor outside approved palette
- Do not distort or rotate
